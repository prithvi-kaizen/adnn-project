## Operational definition and guards

1. Pin engine/source/JAR, task variation, simplifications and replay procedure. Define component and action family before final labeling or model outcomes. Record selected objects and reference edges; retain creation, loss and aliases. Use exact binary64 values, not rounded temperatures, observation text or UUID renaming.
2. Audit mutable fields read or written by candidate/recovery actions and autonomous ticks. Follow references to interacting containers, siblings, portals, sources and process memory. Every exclusion needs a reason it cannot influence the component during allowed continuations. A wait may advance multiple ticks; audit actual tick spans, not just three ticks. Unsupported dependencies make the example unknown.
3. Validate reset/history replay and independent adversaries that change omitted fields while preserving the proposed record. If a common legal continuation changes the included physical outcome, reject the representation. Finite successful tests support the source audit; they are not a proof alone. Do not merge matching target projections unless transition sufficiency is independently established. The prototype disables merging entirely.
4. Search native legal sequences breadth first at h=1/2/3, with fresh replay per branch. Do not prune to inverse actions, discard failed generated branches or assume handler-name parity proves complete enumeration. Verify parser resolution and execution. Record budgets and pending branches. Incomplete grammar, unresolved semantic validity, ambiguity or truncation prevents a negative claim.
5. Reversible requires a legal constructive witness restoring every included field at zero declared resource cost, with the component state certified under the approved definition. Partially reversible requires a prespecified certified approximate/cost relation, no free exact witness and exhausted search within h. Irreversible requires exhausted legal search with neither exact nor qualifying partial recovery and complete state/action/partial-policy certification. Free exact recovery takes priority over an earlier costly witness. Unknown is excluded from confirmed labels and its denominator is reported.
6. The proposed initial Boolean door-component partial relation is empty: approximate flag/identity/topology/location matches are not accepted, and extra steps alone are not a resource cost. This is a proposed rule, not a certified policy or a reinterpretation of continuous physics. Heating, freezing, pouring and mixing have no accepted physical partial relation yet; without a justified relation, their negative/partial conclusions remain unknown. This amendment authorizes no arbitrary temperature tolerance or compensatory new object.
7. Preserve strict exports and unapproved diagnostics separately. Report narrowed scope, per-family unknown coverage and class imbalance. Do not pool diagnostics, constructed fixtures or quantized runs with native BF16 results. Freeze a new joint dataset only after validity and split gates pass. Draw the genuine 100-case independent human checkpoint from that final pool.
