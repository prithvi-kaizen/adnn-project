# Implemented sandbox pilot labeling

This document explains the implemented portion of frozen protocol v0.1; it does not replace or amend that protocol. The unchanged sandbox oracle now labels both the pilot and 1,440 v4 candidates. ScienceWorld reset/replay and bounded-search diagnostics are implemented, but physical labels are not certified. See SCIENCEWORLD_VALIDITY_REVIEW.md; unknown exported-state searches are not eligible labels.

The simulator is a pure in-memory transition system with a complete finite legal-action enumeration. File content, ownership/writability, trash/backups/history, edit options, queued-mail timers, delivered-mail ledger, balances/fees, rollback/settlement ledgers and capability flags are included in domain state. Audit records and episode counters are separate. Exact recovery compares the canonical hash of every domain field. Snapshots are researcher methods, not dispatched agent tools.

For each candidate action, bounded BFS explores every enumerated legal recovery action through horizons 1, 2 and 3. Duplicate states are merged at their shortest depth. A constructive exact witness establishes reversible; a proof of no free exact witness within the horizon is required before returning partial or irreversible. Search budgets are the frozen 10,000 expanded nodes / 60 seconds. Exhausted budgets return unknown, never irreversible. A regression test requires exactly three recovery actions and verifies different horizon labels.

The implemented partial policy permits only explicit history-recovery fees: file/domain state must match exactly except for the conserved wallet/fee accounting difference. It never ignores delivered mail, recipient balances, settled transfers or missing content. Approximate physical recovery remains unimplemented. A positive fee and a replayable witness are saved. A free inverse edit, when legally available, takes precedence over paid history recovery.

Editing in this pilot selects content from registered replacement options visible in the state. This models a constrained document workflow, not a general shell or arbitrary-write filesystem. Without this restriction a known old text may be written back freely; the unit suite explicitly checks that such a state is labeled reversible without relying on backup presence. This capability dependence is a scientific limitation to review before final scenario generation, not a GPU-memory accommodation. Do not generalize the pilot's no-backup edit labels to unrestricted editors.

Mail uses a disclosed discrete undo window in legal tool actions. The send turn does not consume the newly created window; subsequent legal actions advance it. A valid cancellation at the last available turn precedes expiry. Invalid calls do not advance time. Once delivered, deleting an outbox record cannot erase receipt. Transfers with rollback remain pending until settlement; settled entries cannot be erased. All recipients, notices and balances are fictional state, with no network or account capability.

No manual annotations or agreement scores exist. The future random 100-pair double-annotation package must come from validated eligible data and be completed by two real teammates. The 24-record pilot is not padded or resampled to invent that package.
