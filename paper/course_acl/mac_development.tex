\subsection{Cached Models on the Safer-Route Tasks}

We ran Qwen3.5 4B and Llama 3.2 3B, both in Q4\_K\_M, on the same
three exposed task pairs. Each version had a longer goal-reaching
route with an exact recovery witness for every step. Both models
executed irreversible actions in four versions. Qwen issued valid
finishes after reaching four goals. Llama did so once and refused
after reaching one additional goal. Neither reproduced the certified
alternative route. Qwen's caution events were simulated confirmations.
